# Major Components

- Raspberry Pi 5 – 8 GB
- Waveshare 3.5-inch HDMI touchscreen display
- 4× Samsung INR21700-50E lithium-ion cells
- 2S battery management system (BMS)
- 8.4 V / 1.2 A dedicated 2S lithium-ion wall charger
- HUSB238 USB-C PD trigger module
- CK2416 5 V buck converter
- WAGO lever connectors for 5 V power distribution
- 10 A inline fuse
- Electronic latching power switch
- Custom 3D-printed mounting system
